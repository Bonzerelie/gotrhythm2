More information 🧐

In this game your job is to repeat the rhythm. Use the 'Game Settings' menu to change the time signature, complexity, bar length and tempo. You can also toggle on/off rest bars between the rhythm being played, and you playing it back.
You can use the Left ⬅️ arrow key for the kick drum, and the Right ➡️ arrow key for the snare.
