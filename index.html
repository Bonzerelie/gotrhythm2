Welcome


Welcome to Got Rhythm?!

Controls: Left Key - Kick, Right Key - Snare, Spacebar - Pause


In this game you'll be played a rhythm, and then have to play it back. The rhythms will be played on two elements of a standard drum kit - the kick drum (bass drum, a thumb sound) and the snare drum (a higher pitched hit sound). You can play the rhythm back using touchscreen, or if on desktop via the left and right arrow keys - left for the kick drum, and right for the snare.

There are a whole load of game settings you can adjust, such as the tempo, complexity of rhythm, time signature and also whether to include rest bars between the game playing you a rhythm, and you having to play it back. You will get a score based on how accurately you can repeat the rhythms, and you can download a scorecard should you want!
